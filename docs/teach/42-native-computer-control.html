Native computer control

The work began with a signed read-only helper that checked the macOS permission and foreground application. The controller tests then separated the process, window, session, and observation lifetimes.

What happens between approval and the click?

Review can take time. The application might have switched windows, the owner might have revoked the grant, or another observation might have invalidated the reference. The controller rechecks the lease and the helper verifies the exact target before sending the AX action.

flowchart TD
  A[User grants an app and assigns a chat] --> B[Observe the focused window]
  B --> C[Model selects one current control]
  C --> D[Runtime policy resolves approval]
  D --> E{Grant, lease, and target still valid}
  E -->|No| F[Refuse without dispatch]
  E -->|Yes| G[Consume observation and flush intent]
  G --> H[Verify native element and act]

Why does stop release the user before the helper returns?

Stop synchronously revokes the lease and ends the caller's wait. The underlying native call can take longer to settle. The process claim stays held during drain, and a late reply cannot restore the lease or settle an action after ownership has changed.

flowchart TD
  A[Native action in flight] --> B[User stops or takes over]
  B --> C[Invalidate the lease and abort waiting]
  C --> D[Keep exclusive claim until helper settles]
  D --> E[Require new assignment and observation]
  A --> F[Persist confirmed outcome while lease is valid]
  C --> G[Retain unresolved dispatch receipt]
