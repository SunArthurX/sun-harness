Searchable settings

Desktop and web use a shared section registry. Stable section and field IDs support deep links and tests. Host capabilities determine the visible sections.

flowchart LR
  C[Host capabilities] --> R[Section registry]
  T[English and Chinese labels] --> S[Search index]
  R --> S
  S --> U[Route and field focus]
  U --> D[Section draft]
  D --> P[Revision checked save]
  P -->|Conflict| V[Reload and compare]
  V --> D
  P -->|Success| F[Update snapshot]

What survives a conflict?

The form keeps typed values independently of the saved snapshot. Reload replaces the snapshot while preserving the draft. Applying that draft remains a separate action. Returning with Back follows the same dirty-state guard as clicking a section.

How does translation coverage follow the code?

The scanner traverses renderer subdirectories. Moving a settings component into its own folder keeps its English keys and Chinese translations in the normal test gate. Search matches both dictionaries regardless of display locale.
